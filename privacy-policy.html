Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, email, grade level or subject specialization), payment records processed through Paystack, teacher verification documents, class attendance and recordings, and your Telegram user ID when you access ScholaCore through Telegram.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide access to classes, process bursary and tuition payments, verify teacher credentials, deliver Telegram class invites and reminders, and improve the AI tutoring library.

3. Payment Information

Payments are processed by Paystack. ScholaCore does not store your card details; Paystack transaction references are stored to reconcile payment status.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored using Firebase (Firestore and Storage). Teacher verification documents are only visible to the submitting teacher and school administrators.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers necessary to operate ScholaCore (Paystack, Firebase, LiveKit, Groq, Telegram).

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the school administration.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your school's ScholaCore administrator.
